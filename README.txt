BRAND (Keylee brand book v1.0)
Keylee Purple #51325C   Night Black #161618   Key Silver #A8AFB7   White #FFFFFF   Doorlight Green #7E9236
Tints: #EEEBEF (purple 10%), #F6F5F7 (purple 5%), #5C5C5D (black 70%)
Headline 1: Playfair Display Regular 56/64   Headline 2: Playfair Display Regular 40/48
Subhead: Poppins Medium 22/30   Body: Poppins Regular 17/28   Label: Poppins Medium 13px, capitals, 2px tracking
Green line: once per page, in the hero only. Photos and panels: one rounded corner, top left.
Markers: silver menu underline (slides in on hover, stays on the current page) and a short silver line under each section heading, same as the homepage.

PHOTOS (brand book page 39)
Four kinds only: real homes lived in, hands at work, the people behind it, Dubai at eye level.
Avoid: handshakes in suits, over-processed skylines, staged stock families, cartoon houses and keys, strong colours in the frame.
Each photo placeholder names the shot and its export size. Export at 2x, WebP, under 250 KB, object-fit cover.
Home landlord cards 1200 x 640 . Guide cards 800 x 500 . Guide article main photo 2400 x 960 (4:3 crop on mobile) . About founder 960 x 1200
Alt text on every photo. Lazy-load everything below the first screen.
