POA services

We act for you while you’re overseas.

With a power of attorney (POA), we act for you in Dubai: signing, collecting keys, and dealing with the Dubai Land Department (DLD) and service providers. Every action is documented and reported back.

DLD and other offices, attended for you.

What we handle for you

You decide what we can do. We do exactly that, and show you the record.

We only act on what you’ve approved, in writing.

  • Guidance on the POA scope the tasks need, before you sign anything.
  • Acting for you on the specific tasks you approve.
  • Attending appointments and signing on your behalf.

  • A written record of every action, with copies.
