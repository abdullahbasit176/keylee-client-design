Partner brokers

Leasing and sales go through partner brokers

Keylee manages the property and the tenancy, and briefs the broker. The broker runs viewings, listings and the deal. We keep looking after the unit throughout.

Landlord

Owns the unit and pays Keylee a management fee for looking after the property.

Keylee

Manages the property and the tenancy, and briefs partner brokers on each unit.

Partner broker

Runs viewings, listings and the deal, and leases or sells the unit for you.

Tenant or buyer

Pays the broker’s commission once the unit is leased or sold through the broker.
