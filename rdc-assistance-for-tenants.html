RDC assistance for tenants

Your landlord is raising the rent, pushing you out, or won’t fix the unit.

We help tenants in Dubai take rental disputes to the Rental Disputes Center (RDC). We gather the records, prepare the case and follow it through to a decision, with every step on record.

One rule. We never act against a landlord whose property we manage.

When tenants call us

Situations we help with

A rent increase above what RERA allows

Your landlord wants more at renewal than the RERA rental index permits.

Being pushed out without proper grounds

Your landlord wants you out, without the notice or the reason the rules require.

A landlord who won’t do the maintenance

You reported the problem in writing, and the landlord refuses to fix it.

Your deposit hasn’t come back

The tenancy ended, you handed the unit back, and the deposit is still being held.

Services cut off

Water, power or chiller cut off to put pressure on you.

No Ejari registration

The landlord won’t register or renew the Ejari, and you need it.

Charges that aren’t yours

You’re billed for repairs or fees the contract puts on the landlord.

Leaving before the lease ends

You need to leave early, and you can’t agree the terms with the landlord.

A complaint against a broker or agency

Misconduct by a broker isn’t an RDC case. Complaints go to the Dubai Land Department (DLD). We prepare the complaint and the records behind it.

What’s included

What we handle for you

You keep living your life. We do the chasing, the paperwork and the follow-up.

If your case is weak, we tell you before you pay a filing fee.

  • We review your tenancy contract, Ejari, rent receipts and messages with the landlord, and tell you where you stand.
  • We collect the records the case needs: notices, maintenance requests, photos of the unit and payment proof.
  • We check a proposed rent increase against the RERA rental index.
  • We prepare the case file in the form the RDC asks for, and handle the submission and the follow-up.
  • You get an update in writing at every stage, with the documents attached.

Questions

What tenants ask us about the RDC

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

My landlord wants a bigger rent increase than RERA allows. Do I have to pay it?

Not before checking. Increases at renewal are capped by the RERA rental index, and the landlord has to give notice in time. [Confirm the notice period wording before publishing.] Send us the contract and the notice, and we’ll check the numbers.

Can my landlord make me leave because they want to sell?

[Answer to confirm before publishing.] Only on specific grounds and with formal notice. If the notice or the reason doesn’t follow the rules, it can be challenged at the RDC.

The landlord won’t fix the AC. Can I stop paying rent?

Talk to us before you do. Stopping payment can weaken your position. Keep reporting the problem in writing, keep the records, and let us look at filing.

What if Keylee manages my landlord’s unit?

Then we can’t act against them. We check this first and tell you straight away, so you don’t lose time.
