About Keylee

The handover is where we begin.

Keylee is a Dubai property management company built for everything after the keys change hands. Agencies close the deal. Keylee looks after the property, the tenancy and the owner from then on.

Our story

Something we saw from both sides of the door

Our founder moved to Dubai around 2020 and rented, like most people here do. Then he spent years on the sales side of Dubai real estate.

The pattern was the same every time. Agencies start and end with the agent. Once a unit is sold or leased, the deal is done and the owner is on their own.

Nobody’s job started at the handover. So we made it ours.

Keylee is up and running, with clients already on the books.

How Keylee works

Who does what

Landlord

Owns the unit. Pays Keylee a management fee.

Keylee

Manages the property and the tenancy. Briefs partner brokers.

Partner broker

Runs viewings, listings and the deal. Leases or sells the unit.

Tenant or buyer

Pays the broker’s commission.

What we do

  • Property and tenancy management
  • Maintenance and fit-out
  • Snagging and handover
  • POA services and RDC assistance

What we don’t do

  • Act as an agent or run viewings
  • Advertise or list units. Partner brokers do that.
  • Promise guaranteed rent or zero vacancy
