RDC assistance

Your tenant isn’t paying, or won’t leave.

We help Dubai landlords take rental disputes to the Rental Disputes Center (RDC). We gather the records, prepare the case and follow it through to a decision, with every step on record.

When landlords call us

Situations we help with

Rent hasn’t been paid

A cheque bounced, or payments stopped and the tenant has gone quiet.

The tenant won’t leave

The notice period has passed and the unit still isn’t empty.

Security deposit retrieval

The deposit is being held back, or the deductions are disputed.

Maintenance charges

A repair bill, and no agreement on whether the landlord or the tenant pays.

Damage at move-out

The unit came back in worse shape than the move-in inspection shows.

A dispute at renewal

The rent increase, the terms or the notice, and neither side will move.

You need the unit back

You want to sell or move in, and the tenant disputes the notice.

Subletting without consent

Someone else is living in the unit, and you never agreed to it.

A complaint against a broker or agency

Misconduct by a broker isn’t an RDC case. Complaints go to the Dubai Land Department (DLD). We prepare the complaint and the records behind it.

What’s included

What we handle for you

You stay informed. We do the chasing, the paperwork and the follow-up.

If your case is weak, we tell you before you pay a filing fee.

  • We review your tenancy contract, Ejari and payment history, and tell you where you stand.
  • We collect the records the case needs: cheques, notices, messages and photos.
  • We prepare the case file in the form the RDC asks for.
  • We handle the submission and the follow-up for you, under a power of attorney if you’re overseas.
  • You get an update in writing at every stage, with the documents attached.

Questions

What landlords ask us about the RDC

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

My tenant hasn’t paid in three months. What can I do?

Start by collecting what you have: the tenancy contract, the Ejari certificate and proof of the missed payments, such as bounced cheques or bank statements. The dispute can then be filed at the Rental Disputes Center. Send us the records and we’ll tell you where you stand within [X working days].

How long does an RDC case take?

[Typical timeline, from cases you’ve handled.] It depends on the type of dispute and whether the tenant responds. You get an expected timeline with the quote, and an update at each stage.

Do I need a lawyer to file at the RDC?

[Answer to confirm before publishing.] We prepare the case file and handle the submission. If your case needs a lawyer, we tell you early, before you’ve spent anything on fees.

Can you handle it if I live abroad?

Yes. With a power of attorney, we act for you in Dubai while you follow the case from wherever you are.
