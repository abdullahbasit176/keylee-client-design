About Keylee

Are you a real estate agency?

No. We don’t list units or run viewings. Our partner brokers lease and sell, and we look after the property and the tenancy before, during and after.

Do you work with owners who live abroad?

Yes. Owners abroad are one of the two kinds of landlord we’re built for. With a power of attorney, we can act for you in Dubai.

Fees

How much do you charge?

We quote per property, once we’ve seen the unit. Every fee is agreed in writing before we start.

Who pays the broker when my unit is leased or sold?

The tenant or buyer pays the broker’s commission. You pay Keylee for managing the property.

Tenancy and rent

Can you collect the rent and handle cheques?

Yes. We track post-dated cheques, deposit them and send you the receipt.

Do you register the Ejari?

Yes. We register it for a new tenancy and renew it at each renewal.

Can I see my rent and costs online?

Yes. Property management comes with an online owner portal: every payment, every cost with its invoice, your statements and your documents. Log in any time from your phone or laptop.

Maintenance

Who carries out the repairs?

Disputes

Do you handle rental disputes?

Yes, at the Rental Disputes Center (RDC). We gather the records, prepare the case and follow it through. If your case needs a lawyer, we tell you early. RDC assistance

Do you help tenants too?

Yes, with one rule: we never act against a landlord whose property we manage. RDC assistance for tenants
