Before you accept the keys from the developer.

We inspect a new Dubai unit for defects before you accept it, and follow the developer until they’re fixed. You get a written report with photos of every snag.

What’s included

What we handle for you

A proper inspection, a written record, and someone who keeps the developer honest.

Nothing signed until the snags are on record.

  • A full inspection of the unit before the handover.
  • A written snag report with photos, room by room.
  • The snag list sent to the developer in writing.
  • A second inspection once the developer says it’s fixed.
  • Key collection and handover, under a power of attorney if you’re overseas.
